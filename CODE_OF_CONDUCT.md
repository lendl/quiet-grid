# Code of Conduct

Quiet Grid is meant to be a calm place, and that goes for the community around it too.

## Be kind

- Be friendly and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Keep feedback constructive.
- Everyone is welcome, whatever their background, identity or experience.

## Not okay

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Spam or deliberately derailing a conversation

## What happens

Comments, issues or discussions that break these rules may be edited, hidden or removed. Repeated or serious cases can lead to a ban from the project.

## Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, email **quiet-grid@outlook.com**. Reports are kept private.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else people take part in Quiet Grid.
