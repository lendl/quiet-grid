<p>Quiet Grid is a collection of logic puzzles for Android. No ads, no tracking, no internet connection required — everything runs entirely on your device.</p>

<p><b>Puzzles included:</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Chimp Test</b> — Tap the numbers in order before they disappear.</li>
<li><b>Takuzu</b> — Fill a grid with 0s and 1s: each row and column must have equal counts, no three in a row, and no two rows or columns may be identical.</li>
<li><b>Nonogram</b> (Beta) — Deduce which cells to fill based on number clues along rows and columns.</li>
<li><b>Minesweeper</b> — Classic grid puzzle. Uncover all safe cells without triggering a mine.</li>
<li><b>Sudoku</b> — Place digits 1–9 so every row, column, and 3×3 box contains each digit exactly once.</li>
<li><b>Word Search</b> — Find hidden words in a grid of letters.</li>
<li><b>Block Fill</b> (Beta) — Drag pieces onto the grid and clear full lines.</li>
<li><b>Word Guess</b> — Guess the hidden word in six tries.</li>
</ul>

<p>Each puzzle type offers Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert difficulty levels.</p>

<p><b>Privacy by design:</b></p>
<p>Quiet Grid does not use the internet, request dangerous permissions, or collect any data. All progress and settings are stored locally on your device.</p>

<p>Available in English, Dutch, German, French, and Spanish.</p>
