Version 1.14.0

- New: Journey. Chapters of puzzles that slowly get harder, with up to 3 stars per stop. Available in 8 games, from Sudoku to Arrow Escape.
- Animal Doku: hints and the solve walkthrough explain "what if" steps one at a time.
- Arrow Escape: bigger, harder Expert puzzles.
- Challenger: Takuzu no longer ends after three mistakes, and Sudoku, Takuzu and Word Guess play like normal puzzles.
- Bug fixes and stability improvements
